## Overview

This is the accepted paper talk for **Nugget: Portable Program Snippets** at HPCA 2026, the premier venue for computer architecture research.

## Presentation Topics

- LLVM IR-based sampling framework design
- Portable program snippet generation and reuse
- Cross-ISA and cross-microarchitecture portability
- Hardware-based validation of sampling methods
- Performance results on SPEC CPU2017, NPB, and LSMS workloads
- Diagnosis of simulator inaccuracies in gem5

## Key Results

- **100x+ speedup** in sampling overhead compared to functional simulation
- **Binary-independent intervals** for cross-ISA reuse
- Enables fast, hardware-based validation of sampling methodologies
- Helps diagnose simulator modeling errors
